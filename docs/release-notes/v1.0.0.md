# Signal Loom v1.0.0

Everything new since **v0.3.0** (including the v0.4.0 cluster/math/semantic-lanes foundation and the v1.0.0 workspace, inspection, and persistence overhaul).

## Highlights

### Customizable Instrument Cluster
- **5 gadget styles**: **Dial** (arc gauge), **Bar**, **Digit**, **Readout**, and **Lamp** — all freely re-orderable, duplicatable, and bindable to any decoded signal or math channel.
- **Pointer drag-and-drop**: Drag any signal directly from the Signals list onto the cluster strip to add a smart-typed gadget or rebind an existing one (works reliably on Windows WebView2/Tauri alongside native OS file drops).
- **Rich customization & warnings**: Custom labels, min/max scaling, decimal precision, 10 preset colors plus an arbitrary custom color wheel, `WARN` red glow halo/needle/bar alerts on Dials & Bars, and configurable lamp threshold operators (`>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=`, `==`, `!=`).
- **Fast keyboard ergonomics**: Press **`+`** to add a gadget, **`Enter`** (or **Accept ↵**) to confirm, **`Esc`**, clicking outside, or 15 seconds of inactivity to auto-cancel/revert, and **`Del`** to remove an existing gadget.

### Math Channel Studio
- **Expanded expression engine** (shared across Rust `loom-core` and live TypeScript preview): arithmetic, power (`^`), comparisons (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `==`, `!=`), logical operators (`&&`, `||`), conditional `if(...)`, filters (`lp`, `sma`), calculus (`deriv`, `delta`, `integ`), and math functions (`abs`, `min`, `max`, `avg`, `clamp`, `sqrt`, `pow`, `hypot`, `round`, `floor`, `ceil`, `sign`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `atan2`, `exp`, `ln`, `log10`).
- **Guided Studio dialog (`Shift+M`)**: 16 ready-to-use automotive recipes, editable existing math channels, inline autocomplete (`Ctrl+Space`), operator/function chips, searchable signal sidebar with live playhead values, and real-time playhead preview.

### Semantic Scope, Lanes & Custom Signal Colors
- **3-position track selector**: Jump directly between **Overlay**, **Split**, and **Group** (`L` to cycle, or click the segmented selector in the transport bar).
- **7 per-signal visualization styles**: `Auto`, `Step`, `Line`, `Area`, **State bar** (enum ribbon), **Logic flag** pulse band, and **Heat strip** — with compact ribbon lanes beneath analog tracks.
- **Custom signal colors**: Click the color swatch in either the **Legend bar** or **Signals list** to pick from 14 dark-UI hues, choose any custom hex color, or reset to **Auto** ECU-family coloring. Colors propagate across traces, compare traces, frosted lane pills, legend chips, signal swatches, and the raw frame hex readout.
- **Frosted draggable lane pills & dimmed focus**: Translucent rounded pills render cleanly on top of traces; drag vertically (or use `Alt+↑`/`Alt+↓`) to reorder lanes, and click any lane pill or legend chip to focus it while dimming—without hiding—other lanes.
- **Housekeeping cleanup**: Checksums fold into their parent message's lane instead of spawning standalone lanes, with `CHK`/`CNT` badges and a one-click `CHK` hide filter in the Signals sidebar.

### Precision Inspection & Magnifiers
- **2-level Hover Lane Magnifier**: Pause briefly over any lane to reveal a dedicated, lazy-loaded 2-tier micro-scope (`L1 · 4×` with cyan bracket + `L2 · 16×` with individual CAN sample dots) that magnifies **even past the normal 1 ms max zoom floor** down to 10 µs. Stays stably docked away from the cursor/crosshair, prefetches high-res samples before frame 1 (zero tentative settle), supports **`↑`/`↓`** to rapidly A/B switch lanes at a locked timestamp, **`Esc`** to dismiss, and **`V`** / the **Loupe** toggle button to enable/disable.
- **Automatic Timeline Micro-Map Loupe**: Hovering the timeline while zoomed in tightly opens a magnified micro-map above the timeline for precise viewport dragging, scrubbing, and wheel zooming.
- **Zero-jitter telemetry & transport**: Fixed-width frame prefix with signal-colored hex payload and message name at the end (no horizontal jumping), slow-down & speed-up playback controls (`0.1×`–`16×`, `<`/`>` or `Shift+R`/`R`), cursor-avoiding crosshair readout, and floating top-center toast notifications with zero layout shift.

### Landscape-First Workspace & Command Palette
- **Fuzzy Command & Shortcut Palette (`Ctrl+K` / `?`)**: Search every command, keyboard shortcut, and live signal in one place.
- **SOTA keyboard workflow**: `W`/`S` zoom, `A`/`D` pan, `F`/`0` fit all, `Z` zoom to cursors, `G` center playhead, `,`/`.` frame step, `<`/`>` playback speed, `M`/`B` bookmark, `N` note, `1`/`2`/`3` cursors, `L` track mode, `V` lane magnifier, `+` add gadget, `Ctrl+B` collapse sidebar, `Shift+G` hide cluster bar, `F11` maximize/restore.
- **Frameless desktop window & hover-peek rail**: Custom Tauri titlebar (`decorations: false`) with integrated Minimize / Maximize-Restore / Close controls, collapsible left Signals sidebar that peeks on left-edge hover and pins on click, and optional cluster strip hide for maximum vertical plot area.

### Complete `.loom` Layout Persistence & Developer Ergonomics
- `.loom` project files now save and cleanly sanitize your entire workspace layout: **cluster gadgets (order, styles, bindings, colors, thresholds)**, **custom signal colors & trace styles**, **track layout mode (`overlay`/`split`/`grouped`), manual group assignments (`T1`/`T2`), custom lane order**, and **panel toggles** (`railCollapsed`, `clusterHidden`, `hideHousekeeping`), while remaining backward-compatible with older v1 `.loom` files.
- Configurable dev server ports via `LOOM_UI_PORT` and `LOOM_ENGINE_PORT` environment variables (avoiding Windows Hyper-V/WinNAT port reservation conflicts).
